If the water may be unsafe · in this order
1 · Check, then STOP if…

…officials say do not drink, or it smells of fuel or chemicals. Boiling, bleach and filters can’t fix that. Use another source.

2 · Sealed bottled water first

For drinking, cooking, brushing teeth, washing food, and baby formula.

3 · Boil Best method

Cloudy? Let it settle or strain through a clean cloth first. Rolling boil 1 minute. Above 6,500 ft: 3 minutes. Cool, then cover.

4 · Bleach No heat? Do this

Plain unscented bleach, 5–9%. Never scented, splashless, or mixed with cleaners. Clear: 8 drops/gallon. Cloudy: 16. Stir. Wait 30 min.

5 · Filter Only if labeled

Use one labeled to remove parasites. Most don’t stop bacteria or viruses, so boil or bleach after.
