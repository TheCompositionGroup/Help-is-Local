DANGER
FIRST

Fire, injury, rising water, unsafe building: get out, then call 911. Follow official evacuation or shelter orders. Supplies can be replaced. People can’t.

LEAVE IF: evacuation order · fire · flooding · structural damage · too hot or cold indoors · you can’t support a medical need.
