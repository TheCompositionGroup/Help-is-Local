DANGER
FIRST

Fire, injury, rising water, unsafe building: get out, then call 911. Follow official evacuation or shelter orders. Supplies can be replaced. People can’t.

1 · Get safe
Count everyone and pets
Shoes, keys, glasses, meds on you
Smell gas? Leave. Call from outside.
No generator, grill or camp stove indoors or in a garage
2 · Secure basics
Use stored water first; see 002
Set out food that needs no cooking
Flashlight and radio where you can find them
Charge phones and power banks now
3 · Stay connected
Phones jammed? Text. It often gets through.
