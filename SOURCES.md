## Needs a source confirmed

Not independently checked by the build tooling. The agency sites could not be opened directly in the build session; wording was checked only against search summaries.

- **Red Cross 7-day medications:** the Red Cross survival-kit page lists "Medications (7-day supply)", but newer Red Cross checklists list a 1-month supply. The sheets say 7 days. Decide before the next revision.
- **Exact quotes:** "If you ever smell natural gas, Get Out!" (Ready.gov) and "Get out, stay out, call 911" (USFA) were not confirmed word for word. Search summaries support the substance of both.
- **Rising water / injury → get out:** not covered by the USFA fire page. Confirm a source or narrow the line.
- **Phones jammed? Text.** Check against Ready.gov, Make a Plan, or Power Outages.
