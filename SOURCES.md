## Instruction map

### 002 Water First and the 1 · 8 · 30 card (003)

| Instruction on the sheet | Source |
|---|---|
| Rolling boil 1 minute; above 6,500 ft, 3 minutes; settle or strain cloudy water first; cool and cover | CDC, How to Make Water Safe in an Emergency |
| Bleach: plain unscented, 5–9%; 8 drops per gallon clear water, 16 if cloudy; stir; wait 30 minutes | CDC, How to Make Water Safe in an Emergency |
| Never scented, splashless, or mixed with cleaners | CDC, How to Make Water Safe in an Emergency |
| If officials say do not drink, or water smells of fuel or chemicals, boiling/bleach/filters can't fix it; use another source | CDC, How to Make Water Safe in an Emergency |
| Filter only if labeled to remove parasites; boil or bleach after | CDC, How to Make Water Safe in an Emergency |
| Sealed bottled water first | CDC, How to Make Water Safe in an Emergency |
| 1 gallon per person per day; 3 days minimum; 2 weeks if you can | Ready.gov, Water; Ready.gov, Build a Kit; CDC, How to Create an Emergency Water Supply |
| Add more for pets, heat, nursing or sick people | Ready.gov, Water; CDC, How to Create an Emergency Water Supply |
| Sealed bottled or food-grade containers; cool, dark place away from fuel and chemicals; label date and replace home-filled water every 6 months | CDC, How to Create an Emergency Water Supply |

### 001 The First 72

| Instruction on the sheet | Source |
|---|---|
| Stock for at least 3 days; kit items (water, no-cook food, flashlight and batteries, radio, first aid, can opener, whistle, map, charger and power bank, cash, copies of documents, blanket, sanitation, pet/baby/care supplies) | Ready.gov, Build a Kit |
| Meds, 7+ days (a month if you can) + list | American Red Cross, emergency supplies. Red Cross materials show both: 7-day supply (survival-kit page) and 1-month supply (newer checklists). The sheet uses 7 days as the floor and 1 month as the goal. |
| Follow official evacuation or shelter orders; write down official instructions | Ready.gov, Make a Plan |
| Fire, injury, rising water, unsafe building: get out, then call 911 | Fire: U.S. Fire Administration, Home Fire Escape Plans. Rising water: Ready.gov, Floods. 911 as the U.S. emergency number: FCC, Tips for Communicating in an Emergency; USFA. |
| Phones jammed? Text. | FCC, Tips for Communicating in an Emergency |
| Smell gas? Leave. Call from outside. | Ready.gov, Power Outages |
| Charge phones and power banks now | Ready.gov, Power Outages |
| Use stored water first; see 002 | Internal cross-reference to sheet 002 |
| Out-of-area contact; meeting places; check in; text when phones are jammed | Ready.gov, Make a Plan |
| No generator, grill or camp stove indoors or in a garage | CDC, generator carbon monoxide safety |
| Water: 1 gal/person/day | See the 002 rows above |
